What depends on real data

AreaWhat the design assumes
Branch and ATM feedPer location: id, type (branch or ATM), name, address, phone, coordinates, weekly hours, service flags, and an updated-at time. The service list is exactly: cash deposits, business banking, forex, 24h ATM, wheelchair access, parking. ATMs need a separate “accepts deposits” flag. All content here is sample.
Hours and open stateOpen, Closing soon and Closed are computed on the device from weekly hours in South Africa time, not from a server flag. “Closing soon” is under 60 minutes (to confirm). Split shifts and lunch closures aren’t designed yet.
Holiday calendarThe public-holiday note on detail is static text here. In production it needs a dated override list per location, plus national holidays, with a clear “Special hours” row that replaces the normal day. Never show “Open” on a holiday without it.
Location consentThe pre-permission screen is ours, the browser prompt is the system’s. Copy must match the privacy notice and POPIA position, including whether coordinates leave the device for sorting. Recents and saved branches live on the device; confirm that with privacy.
DistanceStraight-line from the user or searched point, sorted nearest first. Directions apps provide real travel time; the design deliberately shows no wait or queue times.
SearchNeeds suburb, town and postcode gazetteer with autosuggest. Suggestions group as place, then ATM or branch by name. Minimum 2 characters.
OfflineLast results and saved branches cached, with a saved-at time. Map tiles may be partial. Directions and Call still work.
LanguagesEnglish, Afrikaans, isiZulu. Strings here are drafts and need professional translation and a pass at 200% text. Number format: space for thousands, comma decimal in Afrikaans and isiZulu.
Map providerNot chosen. The style needs a quiet, low-saturation base with controllable label colour, plus clustering and pin theming.
Report incorrect infoSends location id and a free-text note. Destination and response promise are a business decision.
